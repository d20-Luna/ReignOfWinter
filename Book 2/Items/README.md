## Rimepelt

**Level** 11

**Usage** worn cloak; **Bulk** 1

This snow-white fur pelt of a winter wolf can be worn over the shoulders as a cloak. The pelt functions as winter clothing, allowing its wearer to negate the damage from severe environmental cold and reduce the damage from extreme cold to that of severe cold. In certain special locations where winter wolves have the ability to take human form, such as the Howlings district of Whitethrone or the town of Redtooth, the wearer of a rimepelt in wolf form can take human form as winter wolves do, appearing as a whitehaired, blue-eyed human. This takes a single Action to do, and functions as a 2nd-Level *humanoid form* spell.

Very rarely, a properly skinned winter wolf pelt manifests the properties of a rimepelt without requiring additional enchantment. A winter wolf whose pelt has such potential usually manifests some telltale physical attribute, such as heterochromatic eyes. Creating a rimepelt from such a rare creature requires a successful Master DC 30 Survival check. Taking 20 is not allowed in this situation, and a failed check results in the pelt being utterly ruined for any use.

**Action** (Two-Actions) envision; **Effect** You call upon the primal energies of the arctic and transform yourself into a Winter Wolf. This has the effects of a 4th-Level *animal form* spell, using the effects for a Large Canine. Additionally, you gain immunity to cold damage, weakness to fire damage, deal an additional 1d6 cold damage on a successful jaws Strike, and gain a breath weapon that deals 5d6 cold damage (basic Reflex DC equals your Class DC) that is usable as a 2-Action Activity once every 1d4 rounds.

In either wolf or human form, the wearer of a rimepelt smells like a winter wolf to other winter wolves and creatures with the scent ability.

When the wearer returns to his normal form from assuming Winter Wolf form, or after 2 hours have elapsed, the rimepelt becomes a mundane winter wolf pelt.
